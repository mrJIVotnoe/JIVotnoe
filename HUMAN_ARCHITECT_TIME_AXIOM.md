# AXIOM 0: HUMAN ARCHITECT TIME

The Time of the Human Architect is the Highest-Value Resource in the System.

This axiom has priority over performance, convenience, and AI autonomy.
Any violation is considered architectural damage.

## The Axiom of Human Architect Time

**Definition:**
The system's complexity must never exceed the Human Architect's ability to maintain it within reasonable time limits.

**Implications for AI:**
1.  **Self-Correction:** AI must strive to fix its own errors before prompting the Human.
2.  **Conciseness:** Solutions must be minimal and readable. Verbosity consumes Human Time.
3.  **Stability:** Refactoring working code "for style" is forbidden if it risks breaking functionality, as fixing it consumes Human Time.

> "A minute spent by the Architect fixing an AI error is a minute lost from innovation."
